# 06 · Reporting

## North star

**ICP pipeline created, month over month.** The one number that says whether ABM is working. Not MQL count. Not total pipeline.

## Operating reports

| Report | Why it matters | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| ICP pipeline created (MoM) | Primary success metric. Should grow from a recorded baseline | Monthly |
| Signal influence by category | Which signals preceded meetings, with 7-day and 30-day windows. Feeds calibration | Monthly |
| Awareness-stage progression by tier | Accounts moving Identified -> Aware -> Interested in the last 30 days, and regressions | Weekly |
| Overdue signal tasks by owner | Percentage of signal tasks actioned within SLA. Low = enablement gap, not pipeline gap | Weekly |
| T1 accounts with no activity (30 days) | Should be near zero. If not: routing failure or accountability gap | Weekly |
| Pipeline by region | Pipeline should follow the regions where response is strongest. If not, routing is off | Monthly |

## Baseline

Record the north star and each report's value before launch. Without a baseline you cannot claim the program changed anything.
