# 05 · Plays by tier

Demand generation layered on the ABM infrastructure. Every channel feeds back into the score.

| Channel | T1 | T2 | T3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold email | Rep, one-to-one, researched | Sequencer, signal-lane copy | Sequencer, segment copy |
| LinkedIn | Rep connection + DM | Automated connection + DM sequence | Connection request only |
| Paid | Matched-audience ads on the TAL | Matched-audience ads | Retargeting of Aware and Interested |
| Content / SEO | Personal invites to events and assets | Nurture from subscriptions | Nurture from subscriptions |
| Investor / partner network | Ask for warm intros to named accounts | Monthly update with explicit asks | None |
| Self-serve | Rep follow-up at day 7 | Automated activation nudges | Automated activation nudges |

## Play details

### Cold email

- Priority regions first (`{{regions}}`).
- Conservative daily caps per mailbox.
- A/B variants run as matched cells; promotion on bookings per 1,000 sends.

### LinkedIn

- Free play: connection requests from relevant team members to every T1 decision maker and champion.
- DM sequences triggered by profile views and accepted connections.

### Content and SEO

- Subscription triggers a three-step nurture (day 1, 7, 14).
- Comparison pages ("{{product}} vs {{competitor}}") with a direct booking CTA.
- One pillar page on the category idea you want to own.
- Look for an uncontested angle: a widely used framework or metric in your space that buyers quietly dislike.

### Paid

- Upload the TAL as a matched audience. Spend only on ICP accounts.
- Retarget Aware and Interested.
- Judge paid on multi-touch influence on ICP pipeline, not last touch.

### Investor and partner network

- Frame it as portfolio value-add, not a favour.
- Monthly update with one specific ask: "We just signed a company like X; do you know anyone like Y?"
- Any reply creates a deal with the introducer recorded as source.

### Self-serve (if applicable)

- Free tier or trial as top of funnel.
- Activation score drives rep involvement at day 7.
- No-show recovery: two automated emails.
- Auto-close stale trials after `{{N}}` days, with a warning first.

## Re-engagement: do not fear burning the TAM

- Closed-lost for product reasons: re-enrol at 30 days with a "what changed" sequence.
- Closed-lost for timing or capacity: re-enrol at 60 days.
- These are automated sequences, not just reminder tasks.
- T1 accounts with no activity in 30 days appear in a weekly accountability report.
- Event CTAs (webinar invite) can beat demo CTAs in cold outbound; test it.
